NUMERA

The Shattered Isles of Number

Long ago, the archipelago of Numera hummed with living mathematics. Tides added themselves, mountains kept perfect ratios, and the stars overhead were constellations of tame number-spirits called Numen.

Then came the Null — a silence that unsolved the world. Island by island it drank the knowing out of things, and the Numen scattered into the dark between the stars.

You are a Mathfinder. Every problem you solve returns a little light. Master an art, and its Numen will return to you. Restore every isle, gather every spirit, and face the Null itself.
